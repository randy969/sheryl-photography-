About the artist

Sheryl Ross

Sheryl Ross is a local artist that captures the simple beauty in flowers throughout NJ and beyond. She brings a simplistic view, yet personal approach to the beauty in nature that surrounds us all. Sheryl’s goal is to share her artwork in a way that evokes emotions of the heart and brings peace and relaxation to rest our busy minds. She hopes you enjoy her artwork of a celebration of colors, as much as she enjoys creating it.

Often uses Macro Mode to enhance close up vibrant details, providing a unique perspective intended to awaken passion and excitement.

Inspired by my wonderful husband Alan E. Ross who taught me the Art of Lighting, while he captured creative fine photography at Bar-Mitzvahs and Wedding Day special events.

Inspiration also comes from Barbara Sue and Daniel McKenna who sell their one-of-a-kind framed monotypes in shows throughout the country, and to communities who want to bring Art into their homes.

Thank you to all my dear family and friends who provide Love, constant support, and tremendous encouragement. I appreciate each and every one of you.

In her spare time, Sheryl enjoys spending time with family, volunteers at community events, works with adults who have special needs, and also teaches Hebrew.

With blessings, gratitude, and love,
Sheryl Ross
